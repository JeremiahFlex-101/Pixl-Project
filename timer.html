Focus Timer

Use the Pomodoro technique: 25 minutes of focused study followed by a 5-minute break. Stay consistent and watch your knowledge grow.

Completed focus sessions are automatically added to your study time on the dashboard.

🍅 How it works

1. Choose a task
2. Work for 25 minutes
3. Take a 5-minute break
4. After 4 sessions, take a longer break

💡 Tips for success

Put your phone away, close extra tabs, and focus on one subject at a time. Small consistent sessions beat marathon cramming.
