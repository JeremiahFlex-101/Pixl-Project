About StudySphere

Empowering secondary school students to take control of their learning.

Our Mission

StudySphere exists to make high-quality study tools accessible to every secondary school student. We believe that consistent, active practice — not just passive reading — is the key to deeper understanding and better grades.

What We Offer

  • Interactive Quizzes – Instant feedback helps you identify strengths and gaps quickly.
  • Flashcards – Built on the science of active recall and spaced practice.
  • Focus Timer – Pomodoro sessions that train concentration and prevent burnout.
  • Progress Tracking – See your streaks, scores, and growth over time (stored privately on your device).

Privacy First

No accounts. No tracking. No cloud sync. Everything stays in your browser’s local storage. You can reset your data anytime from the Dashboard.

For Students, By Design

Secondary school is a critical time for building study habits that last a lifetime. StudySphere is designed to be simple, encouraging, and free of distractions so you can focus on what matters: learning.
